The Root

Rooted in Tradition, Rising in Strength.

Kalariwoman is an initiative by Amrutha E.T., granddaughter of the legendary Veerasree Sami Gurukkal. Every movement, stance, and breath on this path is built upon decades of red-earth training, ancient discipline, and an unbroken commitment to the martial heritage of Kalaripayattu.

Kalari was never merely an extracurricular activity. It was a way of life, a language of survival, grace, and spiritual grounding.

The full story

The practice

Vadivu, chuvadu, breath. In that order.

Every stance (vadivu), step (chuvadu), and breath is taught with deep respect for the lineage, focusing as much on internal mindfulness as external power.

Body mechanics

Foundation

Foundational animal postures, flexibility and conditioning, trained barefoot on the floor until the body no longer needs to be told.

Mindful movement

Breath and focus

Movement timed to breath from the first lesson, which is why the practice settles the mind as much as it strengthens the legs.

Traditional weaponry

Once the body is ready

Wooden forms including the ottakol, then metal: sword and shield, dagger. Taken up only after the foundation is honest.

Share Your Shakti

A floor that was never meant to feel intimidating.

For too long, martial arts spaces have felt intimidating or inaccessible. Kalariwoman changes that narrative by offering a nurturing yet rigorous environment where women of all ages can step onto the kalari thara.

Kalari isn't just about movement; it's about reclaiming your strength, sharp focus, and absolute grace.
