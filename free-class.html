You already carry more than you know.

Most days you hold a version of yourself back. Kalaripayattu was built for the opposite of that: strength that does not apologise, focus that does not waver, a body that finally feels like it belongs to you again.

To the women looking to break boundaries, connect with an ancient lineage, and build a body that feels unstoppable — this is your sign.

But the floor can feel very far away.

I have never done a martial art.
Every student on that floor started there. The free class assumes nothing.
The nearest kalari is an ocean away.
This one is not. It comes to your living room, over video, live.
I am not sure my body is "the type" that does this.
Kalaripayattu was shaped by generations of bodies, all different. Yours is one of them.

One free class. Live. Online. Yours.

Once, before you commit to anything, Amrutha teaches a complete beginner class over video call, exactly as she would on the kalari floor: breath, stance, and the animal postures that build the whole foundation of the practice. It is a first, honest taste of a discipline that has moved through her family for generations, not a sales call.

You need a phone or a laptop with a camera, clothes you can move in, and roughly the space of a yoga mat. Nothing else.

This is not a trend. It is a lineage.

Amrutha is the granddaughter of Guru Veerasree Sami Gurukkal. She trains at Hindustan Kalari Sangham, on the same red-earth floor, under the same oil lamps, in a tradition considered one of the oldest fighting systems in the world.

This very floor is where I took my first steps, and over the years, it has become a part of who I am.

What one class actually gives you.

  • Breath and stance. The two things every other technique in the art is built on.
  • Focus under pressure. Trained through repetition and attention, not force.
  • Flexibility and control. From postures modelled on real animals, still practised as they were centuries ago.
  • A living tradition. Technique passed hand to hand from a living teacher, not from a manual.
  • A place to keep going. If it resonates, regular online and offline classes continue from exactly here.
