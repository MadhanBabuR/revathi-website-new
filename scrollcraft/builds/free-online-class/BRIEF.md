# BRIEF — Free Online Class landing page

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The client (via the site
owner, relaying a conversation with Amrutha E T) asked for a landing page
combining the benefits/advantages of her Kalaripayattu training with a call to
register interest in a free online class, explicitly asking me to "come up
with the best version" — i.e. use my judgment rather than run a full
interview. Answers below are authored from the brand's existing, established
voice (the live `index.html` on this repo) plus the real photography already
in `images/`, not invented.

## The eight

1. **Vibe**: reverent, unhurried, earned-not-hyped. References: the existing
   site's own tone (spare, second-person, quiet confidence); the actual
   photography's palette (red-oxide kalari floor, oil-lamp night ritual).
2. **Journey** (see below).
3. **Energy curve**: calm open → a named doubt → warm relief/permission → hushed
   reverence (peak) → clear, plain-spoken benefits → quiet resolve at the close.
   Not loud anywhere; the one moment of heat is emotional, not volume.
4. **Feeling curve + peak**: see below.
5. **The one thing no one else's site does**: a footprint trail. A fixed
   margin rail stamps one bare footprint per chapter as the visitor arrives at
   it, echoing Amrutha's own real quote ("this very floor is where I took my
   first steps") and Kalaripayattu's barefoot practice. It doubles as the
   chapter folio the grammar asks for.
6. **Range**: editorial, not premium-minimal-default and not maximalist —
   "long-form substance" per the chaptered-editorial grammar, because this
   page's job is to be read and believed, not watched.
7. **One world or distinct scenes**: distinct chapters, hard cuts. There is no
   single continuous place to travel through here; the content is sequential
   argument (recognise → doubt → offer → proof → benefit → commit), which is
   exactly what the chaptered grammar is for.
8. **Assets already available**: all real. Existing site photography only
   (`images/post-1..7-hq.jpg`, `images/profile-hq.jpg`), no generation, no
   spend. `profile-hq.jpg` is visually a guru blessing a young student, not a
   headshot of Amrutha — captioned honestly as a lineage/ritual moment, not
   relabelled as her portrait despite the existing alt text on the homepage.
   `post-1-hq.jpg` is a group stage performance (a male performer mid-jump,
   plus two others) — captioned about the practice/lineage, never attributed
   to Amrutha specifically.

## Journey (beats)

1. Recognition — she already carries the strength/desire; this speaks to it.
2. Tension — the real, ordinary reasons someone hesitates (no experience, not
   local, not "athletic enough", never tried martial arts).
3. Turn — the offer: one free, live, online class removes every one of those
   reasons at once.
4. Substance — lineage and legitimacy: Guru Veerasree Sami Gurukkal,
   Hindustan Kalari Sangham, her own quote about the floor.
5. Range — plain, concrete benefits of the practice and of the free class
   specifically.
6. Commitment — one clear ask, said once, quietly.

## Feeling curve

- Hero: **welcomed**. A door held open, not a pitch.
- Recognition: **named**. "That's the thing I've been circling."
- Tension: **seen**. The doubt is said out loud before she has to say it.
- Turn: **relieved**. Every reason not to try just got answered.
- Substance (**peak**): **reverence**. Witnessing something old and real,
  passed hand to hand under oil lamps. Largest scroll room, quietest chapter,
  most visual weight.
- Range: **clear-eyed**. Plain facts, no more persuading needed.
- Close: **resolved**. Quiet, certain, one small action left.

**Tell-someone sentence**: "It's the site where a free class page doesn't ask
you to believe a promise, it just walks you onto the floor once, for nothing,
and lets that be the argument."

## Grammar

**Chaptered editorial.** The brief is a founder story building trust toward
one honest ask — "the visitor should feel they read something rather than
watched something" is exactly this page's job, and there is no video, no
product demo, no two-sided comparison and no real catalogue of objects, which
rules out live surface, split stage, gallery and continuous world. Filmic
one-shot is the default drift this skill warns about and nothing here asks for
a single unbroken emotional arc under one camera; rhythmic cutlist would
undersell the reverence the lineage chapter needs; typographic poster would
throw away real, strong photography that exists and should be shown.

## Fingerprint gate

First build in this repository's scrollcraft workspace — `scrollcraft/FINGERPRINTS.md`
does not exist yet, so there are no existing rows to clear. Passes vacuously;
this build's own row is appended after shipping.

## Signature move

The footprint trail (see §5 above). Bespoke JS reading scroll position against
each chapter's bounding box, not a kit device or a parameter change to one.

## Silence

The Substance chapter (peak) is deliberately the quietest and slowest —
minimal copy, held white(dark)space around the ritual photograph — so its
silence reads as reverence, not as a stall in the build.
